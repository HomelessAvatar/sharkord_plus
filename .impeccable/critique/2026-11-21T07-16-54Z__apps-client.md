## Design Specificity Verdict

### LLM Assessment
Sharkord Plus establishes a clear product vision in `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md`: a high-performance, lightweight, self-hosted communication platform inspired by the Pure Neutral Dark aesthetic of Linear and Raycast, providing Discord-muscle-memory navigation without the bloated telemetry or CPU overhead.

However, across `apps/client`, the design system currently oscillates between a verbatim Discord structural clone and a generic shadcn/Tailwind default:
1. **Theming & Color Drift:** While `DESIGN.md` defines a bespoke neutral scale (`#0a0b0e`, `#111216`, `#16171d`, `#1c1e24`), `apps/client/src/index.css` falls back to generic shadcn OKLCH neutrals. In `channel-view/voice/controls-bar.tsx` (L185), the Disconnect button hardcodes Discord's exact brand red (`#ec4245` / `#da373c`), revealing lingering copy-paste DNA rather than deliberate design tokens.
2. **Surface Omissions vs. Commitments:** `DESIGN.md` promises a 4-pane spatial layout. In Web browsers, the 72px server rail does not exist at all (it is an external native window bridge), leaving the web top bar unbalanced. The Right Sidebar does not implement role groupings or activity cards; it is an unranked flat list hard-capped at 100 users.
3. **Desktop-First Blind Spots on Mobile/Touch:** Essential interactions are built assuming mouse hover (`group-hover/voice-stage` for voice controls, `onMouseEnter` for message actions). Touch affordances, edge-swipe hygiene, and 44px hit-box requirements were secondary thoughts.

## Overall Impression
Sharkord Plus delivers a blazingly fast, responsive core engine with rock-solid reconnection handling and zero backend bloat. However, its frontend client suffers from **cross-platform identity fractures**: desktop assumptions (mouse hover, wide screens) have left mobile and tablet users stranded with hidden voice controls, invisible focus rings, and an empty blackout zone on tablet widths (768px–1023px). Unifying breakpoints, enforcing role hierarchy in the member sidebar, and adding mobile-first touch parity will elevate Sharkord Plus to world-class craft.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Tablet Viewport Blackout & Mobile Thread Dead-End
- **What:** The responsive layout collapses between 768px and 1023px: `TopBar` is `hidden lg:grid`, while `ContentWrapper` and `VoiceChannel` mobile headers are `md:hidden`. In voice channels on tablets, no topbar renders at all, trapping the user. Furthermore, tapping `ThreadRepliesButton` on mobile/tablet fails silently because `ThreadSidebar` has `className="hidden lg:flex"` with no mobile sheet fallback.
- **Why it matters:** Breaks the app completely for tablet/iPad users and locks mobile users out of threads.
- **Fix:** Unify headers into a single responsive `AppHeader` component. Provide a mobile/tablet full-screen overlay for `ThreadSidebar` identical to `VoiceChatSidebar` (`lg:hidden fixed inset-0 z-50`).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt apps/client/src/screens/server-view`

### [P0] Desktop Window Resize Snapping Trap & Mobile Safe Area Insets Missing
- **What:** In `is-desktop-app.ts`, `isMobileDevice()` flips to `true` on screen width $\le 1023px$. If a desktop user snaps their window to half of a 1080p screen (960px), desktop server switching and desktop settings instantly disappear. Simultaneously, on mobile Android/iOS, `env(safe-area-inset-top)` is completely missing, and bottom insets are missing in `user-control.tsx` and `controls-bar.tsx`.
- **Why it matters:** Desktop snapping degrades into broken mobile state, while mobile headers collide with camera cutouts and navigation home bars.
- **Fix:** Guard `isMobileDevice()` to never trigger if `isTauriEnvironment()` or desktop API is present. Add `viewport-fit=cover` and safe-area padding to top headers and bottom control bars.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden apps/client`

### [P1] Mobile Voice Stage Touch Traps & Missing Docked Call Bar
- **What:** `ControlsBar` in voice channels defaults to `group-hover/voice-stage:visible` (`opacity-0`), making controls invisible on touch screens without continuous hover. Furthermore, when a mobile user navigates away from the voice channel to read chat, there is no persistent floating or docked call bar showing mute status or a hang-up button.
- **Why it matters:** Mobile users in voice calls experience panic when they can't find how to mute or disconnect while browsing other channels.
- **Fix:** Make `ControlsBar` permanently visible or tap-toggled on touch devices. Add a persistent docked bottom voice banner (`ActiveVoicePill`) when connected to voice outside the voice channel view.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt apps/client/src/components/channel-view/voice`

### [P1] Member Sidebar Structural Collapse & Working Memory Failure
- **What:** `RightSidebar` renders an unchunked flat list of up to 100 users without role grouping (Admins, Moderators, Members), online/offline segmentation, or activity cards, ending with hardcoded `+{count} more...`.
- **Why it matters:** In communities with >15 users, identifying who is an admin, finding online moderators, or scanning member presence is impossible without opening individual popovers.
- **Fix:** Group users by role hierarchy into collapsible category accordions with online/offline separation and localized counters.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout apps/client/src/components/right-sidebar`

### [P2] Inaccessible Focus Rings & Hardcoded Discord Brand Artifacts
- **What:** `IconButton` explicitly declares `outline-none` with no `focus-visible:ring` styles, rendering keyboard focus invisible. Voice Disconnect button hardcodes Discord red (`#ec4245`). Hardcoded Turkish strings (`aria-label="Ayarlar"`, `aria-label="Arama"`) leak into English viewports.
- **Why it matters:** Fails accessibility for keyboard/screen-reader users, breaks internationalization, and pollutes brand identity.
- **Fix:** Add `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring` to `IconButton`. Replace hardcoded hex colors with semantic `destructive` tokens. Move all hardcoded strings to `i18n`.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish apps/client/src/components`

3. **Web Topbar Asymmetry:** On Web browsers, the top-left area is completely vacant, giving an off-center appearance.
4. *Provocative Question 1:* Why should mobile voice channels render a desktop-style video grid with hidden hover controls instead of an adaptive native call canvas with a persistent bottom sheet (like Telegram or Discord Mobile)?
